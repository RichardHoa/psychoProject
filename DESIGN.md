# MÈO Design System — Hand-Drawn & Soft Aesthetic

## Design Rationale & Philosophy

Mental health and psychological counseling spaces must cultivate **safety, humanity, warmth, and calm** — the exact opposite of cold, sterile corporate tech SaaS dashboards.

Inspired by **[Wired.js](https://wiredjs.com/)** and **Rough.js**, this design system adopts a hand-drawn sketchbook aesthetic:
1. **Human & Tactile**: Imperfect hand-drawn borders, gentle sketch double-lines, and organic paper cards provide the comforting feel of a personal journal.
2. **Minimalist & De-cluttered**: High visualization with **strictly 1 primary text concept per card**. Cluttered hashtag badges and micro-icon walls are abolished.
3. **Soft Calming Palette**: Replaces harsh tech backdrops with warm linen canvas (`#FBF9F4`), charcoal pencil borders (`#262E2B`), soft sage, soothing terracotta, and pastel amber.
